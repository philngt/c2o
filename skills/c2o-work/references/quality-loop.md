# Proportionate Quality Loop

Use during authorized production. In review-only work inspect and report; do not repair. This is a feedback loop, not a required number of iterations.

## Select the relevant quality bar

Recover the original request, full deliverables, completion level, approved references, and criteria. Prefer existing project conventions and available specialist skills. Use only applicable portions of quality-profiles.md. Domain guidance supplements the brief; it does not authorize a backend, account system, publication, or extra formats.

## Prove a representative slice when useful

For repeated screens, scenes, documents, or system paths, choose a slice exposing important content, interaction, and failure states. Bring it to the requested fidelity and confirm it can be implemented consistently before multiplying work. Select a creative direction before expensive refinement unless that choice was delegated.

Keep the full coverage list visible to the orchestrator. A representative slice is a quality baseline, not the end of an explicitly larger delivery request. For one small artifact, produce it directly.

## Observe, repair, recheck

1. Produce a version that can actually be inspected.
2. Open, render, run, interact, calculate, or reconcile sources with available safe tools according to the criterion. Source inspection alone cannot prove appearance, sound, or runtime behavior.
3. Compare observations with the contract; identify the most consequential gap rather than generating generic criticism.
4. Repair an objective defect or material quality gap within existing authorization. Route changed purpose to Shape, value/authority choices to Decide, unresolved creative direction to Create, and empirical uncertainty to a bounded experiment.
5. Recheck affected criteria, evidence validity, and relevant regressions. Preserve valid unaffected evidence.

Do not silently install missing tools, use live credentials, spend money, publish, or mutate production to obtain a check. Where observation is unavailable, report precisely what was and was not checked. Simulated users and model judgments are not observed user outcomes.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the contracted quality and coverage are met, a real capability/authority blocker is reached, or further attempts lack new evidence or proportionate value. Respect the actual budget and user constraints; do not impose an arbitrary universal iteration count.

Do not polish endlessly after acceptance. Do not label a budget-limited partial result complete. Preserve completed artifacts and state required gaps, evidence unavailable, and the decision or capability needed to proceed. Continue independent authorized slices when safe rather than blocking the whole task unnecessarily.

## Usable handoff

When independent use is required, provide the minimum opening/running instructions, representative input or example, success/failure signals, and relevant limitations. Perform an agent walkthrough when possible and label it as such. Actual recipient success or business impact requires separate observation.
